About West Industrial

Built on service, accountability, and follow-through.

West Industrial Services LLC is a Virginia-based, veteran-owned small business serving individuals, organizations, partners, and government customers.

Who we are

Founded and managed by Christopher West, West Industrial Services LLC brings together business administration, cybersecurity, operations, and government-contracting coordination. The company is designed to make complicated work easier to understand and easier to move forward.

How we work

We begin with the actual requirement. Then we define scope, identify risks, explain costs, document responsibilities, and communicate throughout delivery. When a project requires specialized capability, we coordinate qualified partners instead of overstating what one company can do alone.
