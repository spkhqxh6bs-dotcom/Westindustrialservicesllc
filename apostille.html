What we do

A guided coordination service

An apostille is issued by an authorized government office—not by West Industrial Services LLC. Our role is to review the information you provide, explain the likely steps, coordinate the agreed submission and return logistics, and keep you informed.

  • Initial document-readiness and destination review
  • Written service quote before processing
  • Instructions for obtaining or preparing an eligible document
  • Submission, courier, and return-delivery coordination when included
  • Status communication based on the selected service

Common document categories

Eligibility and required preparation vary by document, issuing authority, destination country, and intended use.

  • Birth and death certificates
  • Marriage and divorce records
  • School records and diplomas
  • Corporate documents
  • Court and administrative records
  • Notarized personal documents
  • Federal documents
  • Powers of attorney

How the process works

  1. 1
    Tell us about the request

    Provide the document category, issuing state or agency, destination country, and target date. Do not upload sensitive records.

  2. 2
    Receive a review and quote

    We identify known preparation issues and outline service fees, government fees, shipping costs, and estimated timing separately when available.

  3. 3
    Approve and prepare

    If you proceed, you receive instructions for secure document delivery and any corrections needed before submission.

  4. 4
    Coordination and return

    We coordinate the agreed processing route and return method. Government offices retain final authority over acceptance and issuance.

Pricing and timing

Quotes depend on the number and type of documents, issuing authority, destination country, required certifications, shipping, and urgency. West Industrial service fees are identified separately from government, notary, translation, courier, and shipping charges whenever applicable.

No guaranteed government processing time.Agency review, rejection, closures, mail transit, and country-specific requirements can affect delivery dates.

Frequently asked questions

Can you issue an apostille?

No. Apostilles and authentications are issued by the appropriate state or federal authority. We provide independent document support and logistics coordination.

Should I send my original document through the website?

No. The online form is only for basic request details. Do not submit Social Security numbers, identity-document numbers, payment-card details, or document images. Secure delivery instructions are provided only after the request is reviewed.

Do all documents follow the same process?

No. The path depends on who issued the document, where it was issued, the destination country, and how the document will be used.

Is translation included?

Translation, notarization, certification, courier service, and shipping are separate unless they are specifically listed in the written quote.

Can you guarantee acceptance?

No. The receiving country, requesting organization, and issuing government authority make the final decisions.
